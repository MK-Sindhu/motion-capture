What the colours mean

While comparing, each of your hands is drawn green when the average difference between your 15 finger-joint angles and the reference's is below the threshold (20° by default), and red when it is above. The number next to the hand is that difference, in degrees, for the current frame.

Only hand shape is compared, not where your hand is on the neck. The 3D points are MediaPipe's estimates from one camera; fingers hidden behind the strings are guessed.
